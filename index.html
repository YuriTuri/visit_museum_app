About Visit Museums

Welcome to Visit Museums

Visit Museums is your gateway to discovering and exploring the world's most fascinating exhibitions. We connect art lovers, history enthusiasts, and curious minds with extraordinary cultural experiences.

Our Mission

To make museum visits accessible, convenient, and inspiring for everyone. We believe that art and culture should be available to all, and we're here to help you discover your next favorite exhibition.

What We Offer

  • Easy ticket booking for exhibitions worldwide
  • Curated recommendations based on your interests
  • Real-time availability and crowd information
  • Digital tickets with QR codes
  • Exclusive access to special exhibitions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my ticket? ▼

You can cancel your ticket up to 24 hours before the exhibition date. Go to My Tickets, select the ticket you want to cancel, and tap "Cancel Ticket". Refunds will be processed within 3-5 business days.

Can I change my visit date? ▼

Yes, you can modify your visit date up to 48 hours before your scheduled visit. Additional charges may apply if the new date has a different price.

What payment methods are accepted? ▼

We accept Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. All payments are processed securely with 256-bit encryption.

How do I use my QR code ticket? ▼

Show your QR code at the museum entrance. Make sure your screen brightness is turned up. Each QR code can only be scanned once.
